Maya Callahan Photography — Vermont destination wedding photography.

I'm a solo wedding photographer based in Stowe, Vermont. I cover destination weddings across New England and the Northeast — Vermont, Maine, New Hampshire, and occasionally New York state. I photograph around 25 weddings a year, intentionally capped. I have been doing this for eleven years.

My style is documentary. I do not direct people. I do not ask couples to pose, look at the camera on a count of three, or recreate moments. I move through the day as an observer. I am looking for the glance between two people when they do not know anyone is watching, the grandmother wiping her eyes during the vows, the groomsmen laughing at something off to the side. That is the work.

I am not the right photographer for couples who want a lot of posed portraits. I do a maximum of thirty minutes of portraits per wedding, mostly so couples have something to send to relatives. The rest of the day is documentation.

I shoot mostly outdoors, in natural light, often in rain or low light — New England weather being what it is. My editing is natural and film-adjacent. I do not deliver bright, poppy, highly-saturated images. Colors are true to life or slightly muted. Skin tones are warm. Black and white is a significant part of every gallery (roughly 30 to 40 percent).

Weddings are exclusively full-day coverage. I do not do elopements, engagement sessions, or hourly bookings. Full day is ten to twelve hours. Pricing starts at $6,500. Most couples end up in the $7,500–$9,000 range depending on travel. I do not list pricing on the website — interested couples should inquire.

Ideal clients: couples who genuinely do not care about the photography until they see it. They are not obsessing over Pinterest boards. They want someone trustworthy to be present and take care of it. They find out later that the photos are extraordinary.

Voice: I want the website to feel quiet and considered. Not cold. Not corporate. Not full of exclamation points. The opposite of the loud, heavily branded photographers with testimonials everywhere. I want it to feel like the work — patient, observational, present.

The website should have a portfolio/gallery page, a brief about page, a contact/inquiry page, and the home page. That is all. No blog, no packages page, no FAQ list. Simple.
